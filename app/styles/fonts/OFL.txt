Baloo 2 and Mukta (Ek Type, Mumbai) and Kalam (Indian Type Foundry) were
chosen partly for where they come from: Indian foundries, the same family of
type as Bizzing India.

The .woff2 files are the latin and latin-ext subsets as served by Google
Fonts. They are checked in rather than fetched at build time so that the build
is reproducible offline and the app has no third-party origin.
